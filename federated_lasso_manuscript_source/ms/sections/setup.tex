\section{Setup and algorithm}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Notation}

There are $K$ sites. Site $j$ holds $X_j \in \R^{m_j \times p}$ and
$y_j \in \R^{m_j}$, with $m = \sum_{j=1}^K m_j$ and aggregation weights
$w_j = m_j / m$. Records never leave a site. Write
$X = (X_1^\top, \dots, X_K^\top)^\top$ and $y$ for the (never materialised)
pooled data. The local penalised objective at site $j$ is
\begin{equation}
\Lloc_j(\bbeta)
 = \frac{1}{2 m_j}\, \| y_j - X_j \bbeta \|_2^2 + \lambda_j \|\bbeta\|_1 ,
\qquad \bbeta \in \R^p ,
\label{eq:local}
\end{equation}
with a site-specific penalty $\lambda_j > 0$ selected once, before
federated fitting, by minimising validation mean squared error on the
site's own held-out split over a $30$-point logarithmic grid, and then held
fixed. Writing the penalty on the normalised scale $1/(2m_j)$ keeps
$\lambda_j$ comparable across sites of different size. The aggregated
objective the protocol implicitly targets is
\begin{equation}
F(\bbeta) = \sum_{j=1}^K w_j \Lloc_j(\bbeta) .
\label{eq:global}
\end{equation}
For a vector $v$ and threshold $t \ge 0$, $\soft(v, t)$ denotes coordinatewise
soft-thresholding, $\soft(v,t)_k = \sgn(v_k)\max(|v_k| - t, 0)$, which is the
proximal operator of $t\|\cdot\|_1$ \citep{parikh2014}. We write
$\bar\lambda = \sum_j w_j \lambda_j$ and $\supp(v) = \{k : v_k \neq 0\}$.

\begin{lemma}[The implied objective is a pooled Lasso]
\label{lem:pooled}
With $w_j = m_j/m$,
\[
F(\bbeta)
 = \frac{1}{2m} \| y - X\bbeta \|_2^2 + \bar\lambda \|\bbeta\|_1 .
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$w_j / (2m_j) = 1/(2m)$ for every $j$, so the quadratic terms sum to
$\frac{1}{2m}\sum_j \|y_j - X_j\bbeta\|_2^2 = \frac{1}{2m}\|y - X\bbeta\|_2^2$,
and the penalties sum to $(\sum_j w_j \lambda_j)\|\bbeta\|_1$.
\end{proof}

Lemma~\ref{lem:pooled} is elementary but worth stating, because it fixes the
benchmark. The protocol below is an algorithm for a completely standard
problem---the pooled Lasso at penalty $\bar\lambda$---so any discrepancy
between its output and that minimiser is an algorithmic artefact and not a
different estimand. Throughout, $\hat\bbeta_F$ denotes the minimiser of
\eqref{eq:global}.

\subsection{The protocol}

Algorithm~\ref{alg:fedavg} states the procedure studied here. The local
operator is one or more full cyclic coordinate-descent epochs on
\eqref{eq:local}, warm-started at the broadcast vector. For coordinate $k$
at site $j$, with $r = y_j - X_j\bbeta + x_{jk}\bbeta_k$,
\begin{equation}
\bbeta_k \;\leftarrow\;
 \frac{\soft\!\left(x_{jk}^\top r,\; \lambda_j m_j\right)}{\|x_{jk}\|_2^2},
\label{eq:cd}
\end{equation}
the factor $m_j$ appearing because \eqref{eq:local} is written on the
normalised scale while \eqref{eq:cd} uses unnormalised residuals
\citep{friedman2010}. One epoch is one sweep over all $p$ coordinates. Let
$T_j^E : \R^p \to \R^p$ denote the map given by $E$ such epochs at site $j$.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Synchronous federated coordinate-descent Lasso (\textsc{FedAvg-CD})}
\label{alg:fedavg}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\State \textbf{input} local data $(X_j, y_j)$, penalties $\lambda_j$, weights
 $w_j$, local epochs $E$, tolerance $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{tol}}$
\State $\bbeta_0 \gets 0$
\For{$t = 0, 1, 2, \dots$}
  \State server broadcasts $\bbeta_t$
  \For{each site $j$ \textbf{in parallel}}
     \State $\bbeta_{j,t} \gets T_j^E(\bbeta_t)$
      \Comment{$E$ CD epochs on $\Lloc_j$, warm-started at $\bbeta_t$}
     \State site uploads $\bbeta_{j,t}$
  \EndFor
  \State $\bbeta_{t+1} \gets \sum_{j} w_j \bbeta_{j,t}$
   \Comment{aggregation}
  \State \textbf{stop} if $\|\bbeta_{t+1} - \bbeta_t\|_2 < \varepsilon_{\mathrm{tol}}$
\EndFor
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

Two accounting conventions matter for the comparisons that follow. Upload
cost is $R \times K \times p \times 8$ bytes after $R$ rounds, since each
site returns a dense double-precision vector of length $p$. Local
computation is $R \times E$ coordinate-descent epochs per site. These two
quantities move in opposite directions as $E$ grows, and
Section~\ref{sec:results} reports both.
